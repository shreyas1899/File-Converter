Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Strict Zero-Storage & Stateless Standard

Summary: FastConvert is engineered specifically so that we do not own, inspect, store, or sell your files. All files uploaded are processed in a temporary holding directory and permanently deleted from our server immediately upon completion of the conversion.

1. Strict Zero File Retention Policy

When you upload an image for conversion, the file is temporarily written to an isolated directory solely to allow Node.js to stream the data through the conversion engine (sharp and pdf-lib).

The moment the conversion finishes and the resulting binary stream is delivered to your browser, an automated finally routine executes that permanently unlinks (deletes) the temporary file from disk. In the rare event of a failed conversion, the file is immediately scrubbed from disk as part of the error handler.

2. No Databases & No User Accounts

FastConvert operates entirely without a database. We do not support or require:

  • User account creation, logins, or passwords
  • Storage of personal identifiers or payment details
  • Historic logs associating IP addresses with uploaded file contents
  • Retention of original file names or metadata once processed

3. Cookies and Telemetry

FastConvert does not employ persistent tracking cookies, cross-site advertising pixels, or invasive surveillance scripts. Any local session state exists purely in your browser's temporary memory (via Vanilla JavaScript DOM state) and is cleared whenever you refresh or close the tab.

4. Data Transmission and Security

All network communication between your browser and our server is secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). This ensures that your files cannot be intercepted, monitored, or tampered with in transit across public networks.

5. Your Rights and Data Ownership

You retain 100% full intellectual property ownership and copyright of any images or documents you upload. Because we delete files immediately upon transmission, we do not hold any copy of your files that could be subpoenaed, sold, or shared with third parties.

6. Updates to This Policy

Any future revisions to this policy will remain faithful to our foundational stateless promise: zero persistence, zero tracking, and immediate deletion of user data.
